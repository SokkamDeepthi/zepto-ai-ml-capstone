Policy: Order Cancellation

Question: Can I cancel my order?

Answer: An order may be cancelled before preparation or dispatch, subject to the order status and applicable conditions.

---

Policy: Cancellation After Dispatch

Question: Can I cancel an order after it has been dispatched?

Answer: Cancellation may not be available after dispatch. Check the current order status or contact customer support for assistance.

---

Policy: Cancellation Charges

Question: Will I be charged for cancelling an order?

Answer: Any applicable cancellation amount depends on the order status and the conditions associated with the order.
