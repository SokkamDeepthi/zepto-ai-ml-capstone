Policy: Delivery

Question: How long does delivery usually take?

Answer: Standard delivery usually takes 10 to 30 minutes depending on store availability, order size, traffic, and delivery partner availability.

---

Policy: Late Delivery

Question: What should I do if my order is delayed?

Answer: Check the order tracking status first. If the order remains delayed beyond the expected delivery window, contact customer support with the order ID.

---

Policy: Delivery Tracking

Question: How can I track my order?

Answer: Use the order tracking section in the application to view the current order status and delivery progress.
