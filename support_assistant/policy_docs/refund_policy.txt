Policy: Refund

Question: When can I request a refund?

Answer: A refund can be requested for eligible issues such as missing items, incorrect items, damaged items, or an order that was not delivered.

---

Policy: Missing Item

Question: What happens if an item is missing from my order?

Answer: Report the missing item through customer support with the order ID and item details. The support team can review the issue and process an eligible refund.

---

Policy: Refund Processing

Question: How long does a refund take?

Answer: Once approved, the refund processing time depends on the payment method and the financial institution used for the transaction.
