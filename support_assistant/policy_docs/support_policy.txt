Policy: Customer Support

Question: How can I contact customer support?

Answer: Contact customer support through the available support option in the application and provide your order ID and a clear description of the issue.

---

Policy: Order ID

Question: What information should I provide to support?

Answer: Provide the order ID, the issue you are facing, and relevant item details. This helps the support team investigate the problem.

---

Policy: Unrecognized Question

Question: What happens when the assistant cannot find a relevant policy?

Answer: The assistant should clearly state that no sufficiently relevant policy was found and recommend contacting customer support instead of guessing.
